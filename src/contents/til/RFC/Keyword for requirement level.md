When writing requirement or document, it’s important to convey the urgency and importance of the recommendation/requirement. In [RFC2219](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119), it listed out 5 groups of requirement level as follows:

1. **MUST, REQUIRE, SHALL**
    
    absolute requirement; need to be followed
    
2. **MUST NOT, SHALL NOT**
    
    absolute prohibition; DO NOT perform the action
    
3. **SHOULD, RECOMMENDED**
    
    If you ignore it, it needs a valid strong reasons why it should be a exception.
    
4. **SHOULD NOT, NOT RECOMMENDED**
    
    If you need to do it, it needs a valid strong reason why it is required to do so.
    
5. **MAY, OPTIONAL**
    
    May or may not do it. Still it’s a good idea to include the reason why or why not.
    

This will change how I word my ticket and issue when contributing on open-source or I would write my own requirement.
