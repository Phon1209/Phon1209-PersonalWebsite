If the photo has a subject, it’s important not to put the subject in the middle of the photo. It looks awkward. Instead, put the subject at the one-third line either left/right/top/down or the intersection of the two.

If there’s a object of interest as well, try framing it at the another one-third part of the photo. Try cropping the image to different shot size (medium-close up or similar).

Another thing is that try not to have anything stick out from your subject head, it looks weird.
