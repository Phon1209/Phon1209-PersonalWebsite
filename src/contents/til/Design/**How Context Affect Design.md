#fix-image 

There’s 3 step when reader interpret the design:

1. What does the design **verbally** tell you?
    1. including the texts and symbols
2. What does the design **visually** tell you?
    1. including color, typography, imagery
3. What is the overall message?

All of these form a specific message that has to convey message to the user effectively, and ultimately optimize user experience. We shouldn’t add art for the sake of aesthetic, this should be done with a purpose.

Now, what and how to convey is depending on 4 elements from Roman Jakobson’s model of communication:

1. Message: The content itself
2. Context: Shared Situation for mutual understanding
3. Contact: The medium of communication
4. Code: Shared system of language/rule to both addresser and addressee use to interpret the message
