#fix-image


There’s four main sections of WCAG, guideline, quick reference, understanding(explanation), and technique pages.

It’s linked to each other to provide context to each others and mapped to t#he following structure.

![image.png](attachment:6a497789-69df-4cf0-8ed3-0917bf3b5a6b:image.png)

Usually the guideline page will only have the overview of what to do in the form of success criteria but we can learn more on what and why these success criteria are here in the first place from the understanding page.

The understanding page will also link to the technique page on what to do to be conformant. However, these techniques are non-conformance which means it’s recommended but not a must if it can be fulfilled by other means instead.
